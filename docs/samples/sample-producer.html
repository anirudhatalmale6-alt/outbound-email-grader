  • There is no clear ask anywhere in the message.

  • 6 phrase(s) filters associate with bulk mail: "act now", "buy now", "click here", "limited time", "risk free"
    Rewrite these in plain language. Any one of them is survivable; several together is what gets a message scored as promotional.
  • Shortened links hide where they go: http://bit.ly/3xk9zq
    Link to the real address. Shorteners are one of the strongest single spam signals because that is what phishing uses.
  • A mail-merge placeholder went out unfilled: {{first_name}}
    The recipient literally read that. Check the merge before sending, and make the sending tool refuse to send on a blank field.

Smaller things
  • More than one exclamation mark in the subject. One at most, and usually none.
  • 10 exclamation marks in the message. Keep it to one, if that.
  • Runs of punctuation like !!! or ???. Use single punctuation.
  • Manufactured urgency: "act now", "expires today" Urgency that is not real reads as pressure and gets reported as spam more often than it gets replies.
  • 1 link(s) use http:// rather than https://. Use https. Some clients warn on plain http links.
